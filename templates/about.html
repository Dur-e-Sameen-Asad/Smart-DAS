About SmartDAS

Empowering accessibility through intelligent document understanding.

What is SmartDAS?

SmartDAS is an AI-powered platform that extracts, summarizes, and narrates text from uploaded documents and images. It is designed with accessibility in mind, offering crucial assistance for users with visual impairments or those needing rapid information comprehension.

Our Mission

Our mission is simple: to make digital information accessible and inclusive for everyone. We achieve this by leveraging cutting-edge artificial intelligence and natural language processing to break down barriers to information access.

Key Features

  • Text Extraction from PDFs and Images
  • AI-Generated Audio Summaries
  • Smart Keyword and Topic Detection
  • Accessibility-Focused Design and Output
